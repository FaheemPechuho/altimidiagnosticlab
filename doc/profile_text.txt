OUR
SERVICE
HEMATOLOGY 
The Hematology section of the laboratory department is staffed by a team of
highly qualified hematologists and laboratory scientist who use state of the art
analyzers to perform a Wide range of tests, to diagnose and monitor various
hematological disorders. 
MICROBIOLOGY 
It is a specialized clinical laboratory unit that studies microorganisms in blood and
other bodyfluids to detect, identify, and monitor infectious agents. It works by receiving
properly collected blood samples, which are then processed using culture techniques,
staining methods, and molecular or automated assays to identify bacteria, viruses,
fungi, and parasites.
CLINICAL CHEMISTRY
 
The Clinical Chemistry Department of the Pathology Department is the core of
the organization, specializing in performing a variety of laboratory assays. It uses
automated analyzers and standardized biochemical methods to measure
substances such as enzymes, hormones, electrolytes, proteins, and metabolites. 
HISTOPATHOLOGY 
Histopathology department is concerned with the diagnosis and study of diseases
by examining biological tissues at a microscope level , Our trained staff of doctors
and staff specialized in histopathology department examining tissue samples
obtained through a biopsy or surgical procedure
